MY JOB

To build a premium, data-driven environment where complex quantitative trading becomes visually intuitive. My goal was to create a "command center" feel that empowers users to act on AI-driven predictions.

Bridging the chasm between raw quant algorithms and frictionless trader decision-making.
KEY DESIGN PILLARS
🖥️
The Command Center (Dashboard) Information Density

I prioritized information density without clutter. By using a modular grid, I ensured that "Live Predictions" and "Overall Performance" were always front and center.

⚡
Strategy Engineering Visual Logic Builder

The "Visual Builder" was the most challenging part of the UI. I designed it to bridge the gap between complex coding and visual logic, creating a clean, gridded canvas that feels like a professional creative tool.

💎
The Premium Experience Tiered Coefficients

For the "Coefficient Plans," I moved away from boring tables. I designed immersive, glowing cards that make the act of "Upgrading" feel like a reward rather than a transaction.

🛡️
Trust & Reliability Institutional Security

In Fintech, trust is everything. I designed the Settings and KYC sections to be clean, transparent, and authoritative, ensuring users feel their data and funds are secure.
